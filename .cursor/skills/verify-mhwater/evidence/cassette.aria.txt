TOILET WASTE

A cassette is a removable toilet tank you empty at a service point — usually about 15–20 L on UK leisure vans (Thetford-style).

FLUSH HABITS

A typical cassette flush is about 0.25 L. Five flushes each for two people is about 2.5 L a day. These litres go in the cassette, not the grey tank.
